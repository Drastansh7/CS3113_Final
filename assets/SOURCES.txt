Dungeon Cleanup Crew swapping in art from itch OpenGameArt and similar

if they ask

and sprite hitboxes

  assets/textures/tileset.png
  assets/textures/player.png
  assets/textures/slime.png
  assets/textures/ghost.png

player.png two columns four rows eight frames rows are down up left right two walk frames each

slime ghost charger one row four frames left to right
